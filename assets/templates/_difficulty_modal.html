How difficulty is calculated

Difficulty is a train–test distribution-separability proxy. A fixed logistic regression domain classifier predicts whether each observation comes from train or test using a leakage-controlled PCA representation of their common input features.

AUROC comes only from held-out predictions after reproducible, balanced sampling. A value near 0.5 means the domains are difficult to distinguish; a higher value indicates a larger measured shift.

The shift score is 2 × (AUROC − 0.5), clipped to 0–1. Percentile is the Challenge's relative position among successfully evaluated Challenges in the current catalogue.

This is not an absolute difficulty score. It cannot distinguish biological from technical shift and does not predict downstream model performance.
